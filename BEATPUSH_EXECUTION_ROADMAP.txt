BEATPUSH - AI-POWERED MUSIC PROMOTION PLATFORM
COMPLETE EXECUTION ROADMAP
================================================================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
-----------------
BeatPush is an AI-powered platform that helps African music creators (Artists, 
DJs, Producers) distribute music, automate promotion, track analytics, earn 
money through tips/bookings/sales, and manage licensing - all in one place.

CORE VALUE PROPOSITION:
-----------------------
"One Dashboard. All Platforms. Real Results. Powered by AI."

TARGET MARKETS:
---------------
- Primary: Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa
- Secondary: UK, USA, Canada (African diaspora)

REVENUE MODEL:
--------------
1. Distribution fees ($5-10/year per artist)
2. Premium subscriptions ($15/month)
3. Booking commissions (10-15%)
4. Beat marketplace (15-20% commission)
5. Licensing fees
6. Tip transaction fees (2-3%)
7. Advertising & promoted content

   - Respond quickly to support
   - Pay creators on time, every time

   - DJs need producers (get producers first)
   - Artists need DJs (create incentives)
   - Fans need artists (follow the creators)

6. USE AI WISELY
   - Don't over-automate (keep human touch)

   - Continuously improve AI models
   - Be transparent about AI usage
