## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Before creating a fan club, ensure:
- ✅ You have a **Creator account** on BeatPush
- ✅ Your account is **verified** (email confirmed)
- ✅ You have uploaded at least **3 tracks** or **10 posts**
- ✅ You have at least **50 followers**

### Account Upgrade

If you're currently a fan/listener:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Account Type**
2. Select **"Upgrade to Creator"**
3. Complete verification process
4. Wait for approval (usually 24-48 hours)

## 🎪 Creating Your Fan Club

### Step 1: Navigate to Fan Club Section

1. Log in to BeatPush
2. Go to your profile
3. Click **"Create Fan Club"**

### Step 2: Fill Out Details

**Fan Club Name:**
- Keep it short and memorable
- Example: "Afrobeats VIP Club", "Producer's Inner Circle"

**Description:**
- Explain what subscribers get
- Highlight unique value
- Keep it under 500 characters

**Welcome Message:**
- Personalize it!
- Thank new subscribers
- Set expectations
- Example: "Welcome to my exclusive club! You'll get early access to all my music, behind-the-scenes content, and direct messaging with me. Let's create magic together! 🎵"

### Step 3: Create Your Fan Club

Click **"Create Fan Club"** - you can edit everything later!

## 🎯 Setting Up Tiers

### Tier Strategy

You can create **up to 3 tiers**. Think Bronze → Silver → Gold.

**Recommended Approach:**

#### Tier 1: Bronze ($4.99 - $9.99)
- Entry-level access
- Basic exclusive content
- Example benefits:
  - Exclusive posts
  - Early music releases
  - Subscriber-only updates

#### Tier 2: Silver ($9.99 - $19.99)
- Mid-level access
- More engagement
- Example benefits:
  - Everything in Bronze
  - Behind-the-scenes content
  - Exclusive livestreams
  - Priority responses to DMs

#### Tier 3: Gold ($19.99 - $49.99)
- Premium access
- Maximum engagement
- Example benefits:
  - Everything in Silver
  - 1-on-1 Q&A sessions
  - Discord/WhatsApp group access
  - Input on upcoming projects
  - Exclusive merchandise

### Creating a Tier

1. Go to **Fan Club** → **Tiers**
2. Click **"Add New Tier"**
3. Fill out:
   - **Name**: Bronze/Silver/Gold (or custom)
   - **Tier Level**: 1, 2, or 3
   - **Monthly Price**: $2.99 - $99.99
   - **Benefits**: List all perks (add as many as you want)

**Yearly Pricing:**
- Automatically calculated
- 10% discount (2 months free)
- Encourages long-term subscriptions

### Tier Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Start with 1-2 tiers, add more later
- Make benefits clear and specific
- Price competitively ($9.99 is the sweet spot for most)
- Deliver consistent value

❌ **Don't:**
- Overpromise and underdeliver
- Make tiers too similar
- Price too high initially
- Forget to engage with subscribers

## 💰 Pricing Strategy

### Recommended Pricing (2026)

**African Market:**
- Entry: $4.99 - $7.99
- Mid: $9.99 - $14.99
- Premium: $19.99 - $29.99

**Global Market:**
- Entry: $9.99 - $14.99
- Mid: $19.99 - $29.99
- Premium: $39.99 - $99.99

### Revenue Splits

**You Earn: 90%**
**Platform Fee: 10%**

**Example:**
- Subscriber pays: $10/month
- You receive: $9/month
- Platform fee: $1/month

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

**Calculator:**
```
MRR = (Bronze subscribers × Bronze price) + 
      (Silver subscribers × Silver price) + 
      (Gold subscribers × Gold price)

Your earnings = MRR × 0.90
```

**Example:**
- 20 Bronze @ $5 = $100
- 15 Silver @ $10 = $150
- 5 Gold @ $20 = $100
- **Total MRR** = $350
- **Your earnings** = $315/month

## 📊 Understanding Analytics

### Accessing Analytics

Go to **Fan Club** → **Analytics**

### Key Metrics

**MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):**
- Your predictable monthly income
- Track growth month-over-month

**Churn Rate:**
- % of subscribers who cancel
- Goal: Keep below 5% monthly

**LTV (Lifetime Value):**
- Average total revenue per subscriber
- Higher = better retention

**Engagement Rate:**
- How often subscribers view your content
- Goal: 50%+ monthly engagement

### Using Data

**If churn is high (>10%):**
- Survey canceling subscribers
- Improve content quality
- Increase posting frequency
- Add more value to tiers

**If MRR is flat:**
- Promote fan club more
- Add higher-priced tier
- Offer yearly discount promo
- Increase content quality

**If engagement is low (<30%):**
- Post more consistently
- Vary content types
- Ask for feedback
- Host interactive events

## 💳 Payment Setup

### Stripe (Global)

1. Go to **Settings** → **Payments**
2. Click **"Connect Stripe"**
3. Follow Stripe onboarding
4. Verify identity
5. Add bank account

**Requirements:**
- Valid ID
- Bank account or debit card
- Business/personal tax info

**Payout Schedule:**
- Weekly (default)
- Manual (on-demand)

### Paystack (Africa)

1. Go to **Settings** → **Payments**
2. Click **"Connect Paystack"**
3. Sign up on Paystack
4. Complete KYC verification
5. Add bank account

**Supported Countries:**
- Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Egypt

### Webhook Setup

**Important:** Configure webhooks for automatic processing:

1. In Stripe/Paystack dashboard
2. Add webhook URL: `https://api.beatpush.com/api/v1/webhooks/stripe`
3. Select events: subscription.*, invoice.*, charge.*
4. Save webhook secret
5. Add to BeatPush settings

## ❓ FAQs

### Can I change my fan club name?

Yes! Go to **Fan Club** → **Settings** → **Edit**

### Can I delete a tier?

Yes, but only if no one is subscribed to it.

### How do I cancel a subscriber?

You can't directly cancel, but you can refund them which cancels the subscription.

### When do I get paid?

Payouts happen weekly (Stripe) or on-demand (Paystack).

### What if a payment fails?

The system automatically retries 3 times over 7 days. Subscribers are notified.

### Can subscribers pause their subscription?

Yes, for up to 90 days.

### Can I offer free trials?

Not yet, but coming soon!

### How do I handle refunds?

Contact support@beatpush.com for refund requests.

### Can I have more than 3 tiers?

No, maximum is 3 tiers per fan club.

### What if I want to close my fan club?

You must first cancel all active subscriptions, then deactivate in settings.
